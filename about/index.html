The person behind the notes

Hi, I'm Coby.

I'm interested in the practical side of AI and software: how things work, where they help, and what it takes to use them well.

This is a place to share what I'm learning along the way. Some topics need a longer explanation. Others are better as a small example or a two-minute video.

I aim for useful over comprehensive. Something you can understand, try, and come back to later.

What you'll find here

Notes on AI, developer tools, and building software. I'm starting with Two-Minute Token Tips, a short series about getting better results from coding agents without unnecessary complexity.

A note on sources

Technical guidance changes. I'll link to supporting references, make the scope clear, and update notes when something important changes. Examples are examples, not promises that a technique will work the same way in every situation.
